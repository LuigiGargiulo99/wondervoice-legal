# Account &amp; Data Deletion — WonderVoice

You can request deletion of your WonderVoice account and all associated personal
data at any time. There are two ways to do this.

## Option 1 — In the app (immediate)

Open the app and go to **Settings → Delete account**. This permanently removes
your profile, preferences, itineraries, audio guides, downloaded audio files,
notification tokens and activity log.

## Option 2 — By email

Send a deletion request to **galusolutions@gmail.com** from the email address
associated with your account. We will process it and respond within one month.

## What is deleted

Profile and account identifiers, travel preferences, generated itineraries and
audio guides, audio files, push-notification tokens, and activity logs.

## What may be retained

Some purchase-related records may be retained for the period required by tax and
accounting law (up to 10 years), in minimised form, as described in the
[Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html).
